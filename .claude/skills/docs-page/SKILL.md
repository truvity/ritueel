name: docs-page
description: Use when creating or editing a page of the ritueel site under docs/ (not docs/decisions/), including how it says what is built, asides and diagrams.
---

Read `docs/contributing/pages.md` and follow it. For any name, address or id, also read `docs/contributing/public-content.md`.

Checklist:

- [ ] The front matter has a `title` and a `description` of 40 to 160 characters.
- [ ] The first paragraph of a page about a tool says how far it is built, as the page describes.
- [ ] Agreed, proposed and open points are marked the way the page describes.
- [ ] Diagrams keep to the size limits on the page.
- [ ] Links to other pages use site paths, and the linked pages exist.
- [ ] `devbox run -- just check` passes; then follow `docs/contributing/workflow.md`.
