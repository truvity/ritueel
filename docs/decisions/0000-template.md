## Decision

1. What we do, in the active voice: "We use X for Y." One rule in each sentence.
2. The next rule, in the same way.

## Why

The reasons, in a short paragraph or a few bullets. Link the proposal issue.

## What it costs

What gets harder, and what must follow (migration, pages, tests, standards register).

## Roads not taken

- **Option A.** What it would have given, what it costs, and why it lost, in one line.
- **Option B.** The same, for the next option weighed.

## Not yet proven or only proposed

- **Not yet proven.** A decided rule that waits for a test on a card or an account, with its issue ([#1](https://github.com/truvity/ritueel/issues/1)).
- **Proposed.** A point that is not decided, with its issue ([#1](https://github.com/truvity/ritueel/issues/1)).
