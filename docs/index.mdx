ritueel helps one person or a small team keep a private CA root (the top key of a PKI, a system of certificates) or an OpenPGP primary key for years without losing it, leaking it or letting it expire. The tools are planned: a program that guides you through each use of the root (a [ceremony](/glossary/)) and keeps a record of it.

## The ideas

- [One set of ceremonies](/how-it-works/#every-ceremony-has-six-steps) over three kinds of store: a key file, a pair of root cards or a cloud key service.
- [Prefer a key that cannot be copied](/how-it-works/cards/): a root born on a card or in a cloud key service, over a key in a file.
- [One bundle](/how-it-works/bundle-and-stick/) is one trust domain: its roots, its history, its trust files and what is known about the cards.
- [Requests in, answers out](/how-it-works/#sign-an-intermediate): other systems connect through files, and a plan or a request made in one place can travel by any channel and run in another.
- [A defined, automated workflow](/how-it-works/#every-ceremony-has-six-steps): ritueel says what is due, and every ceremony is plan, confirm, reserve, sign, verify, record.

[See all five drawn.](/how-it-works/#five-ideas)

## What exists today

ritueel is in its design phase. There is a design, stated in brief on [The design](/design/), a set of teaching pages, pictures of [how it works](/how-it-works/) and a [demo](/demo/) of the program's local page, with made-up data. There is no program and no release yet. [Where it stands](/status/) says how far each step has got.

## Is it for you?

Yes, if you hold a private CA root or an OpenPGP primary key and use it rarely, by hand, with a person present. No, if you look for a service that signs for you every day. Nothing here needs a tool today: the [Learn](/learn/) pages teach the practice whichever tool you use. Lost or exposed a key? Read [revoke](/learn/revoke/) first.

The words are fixed in the [glossary](/glossary/). The design is stated in brief on [The design](/design/); the records behind it, the specifications, guides and research are working material in the [repository](https://github.com/truvity/ritueel/tree/main/docs), not on this site.
