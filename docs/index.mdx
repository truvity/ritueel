## Our mission

<Mission>
ritueel teaches people to manage the root of a PKI and the master key of OpenPGP smoothly and safely, and gives them tools that simplify each ritual, from creating a root to signing intermediates and subkeys onto cards, while the person stays in charge.
</Mission>

Almost everything built on keys hangs from a few keys that are used rarely: the root of a certificate hierarchy, the master key of an OpenPGP identity. Using them is a manual ritual, a *ceremony*, done by a person, and most people do it once or twice in their life. ritueel exists to make that experience smooth and safe.

## Does this sound familiar?

Two kinds of trouble, and most people meet both in the same afternoon. Both come from one fact: you do this rarely, and the manual assumes you do it daily. Each chip leads to the page that deals with it.

Learning and tools meet in two things. The **ledger** is the written record of what you did: which roots exist, where each lives, what was signed and when. Next year you do not start from memory. Read [how to keep it](/learn/the-ledger/).

The four **rituals** are what you come back to. Each one reads the ledger and adds to it, whether you run it by hand from a guide or with a tool.

</Rituals>

## What comes next

When the root is in order, the next step is identity built on top of it. [sluis](https://sluis.id) holds intermediates and signs online. Your root of custody first, then secure-by-design identity on top. The two are separate products; they do not depend on each other and exchange standard formats only.
