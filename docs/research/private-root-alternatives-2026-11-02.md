## Question

What does a reader who wants a private root today, or an OpenPGP primary key, have besides ritueel, and what does each option do and not do? It feeds [ritueel compared with what you can use today](/learn/compared/).

## When

Read on 2026-11-02, in the morning (UTC). Every quotation was checked against the source text, not against a summary.

## Verdict

Each option below is real, shipped and in use, and each does one part of the job well. Among the pages read, only YubiHSM 2 is described as making a key on the device and attesting it, at EUR 786.50 a device; the others import a key that was made elsewhere, keep it in software, or (AWS KMS) make it inside the service. None of the pages describes a ceremony record kept for a successor. That combination, on a card or a cloud key, is what ritueel designs; it is not built.

## Method

Fetched each page or file with `curl` (or the GitHub API), read the text, and copied the quotation below from it. A figure from a store page is the price shown to an EU visitor on that day. No device was bought or run, and no tool was run: these are statements of the projects, not tests.

## What the setup over- and under-counts

- A project's own description is the project's claim. Where a page says what a tool does, that is what its authors say it does.
- A feature missing from the text read may exist elsewhere. Wherever the table says a thing is absent, it means absent from the named page on that day.
- The price depends on the visitor's region and on VAT.
